Capybara Hop! Privacy Policy

Effective September 11, 2026

Capybara Hop! stores gameplay progress, virtual currency balances, booster balances, outfit ownership, settings, a player-chosen display name, gameplay events, and leaderboard scores. Players are not required to provide their real name.

Cloud services

Supabase is used for authentication, cloud saves, analytics events, and leaderboards. Anonymous player accounts may be created so progress can be associated with one installation.

Advertising

The app may use Google AdMob for optional rewarded advertisements. Ads appear only after a player chooses a reward; the app does not show automatic interstitial or banner ads. Consent choices are requested before the advertising SDK initializes where required. Development builds use test advertisements.

Purchases

Apple or Google processes payments. RevenueCat may validate purchase status and restore non-consumable purchases. The app does not receive complete payment-card details.

Choices

Sound, music, and haptics can be disabled in Settings. Advertising choices can be changed through the consent options made available by the advertising provider.
